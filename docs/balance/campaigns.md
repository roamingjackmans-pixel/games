# Balance report (M15)

Generated by `npm run balance` (tests/sim/balance.sim.ts) with 5 campaign(s) per bot. Each bot starts a new
character of its class and plays zones 1–8 in order: every combat, ambush and treasure room (a death sends it
back to the bonfire and it fights the room again, up to 4 times), rests at the anteroom, then fights the boss
until it falls (up to 12 attempts). It levels up at every rest with its Shards and wears better drops.
Bots react to telegraphs after 0.25 s, dodge Red hits, step out of Piercing areas and use pockets.
Times are simulated seconds of fighting (no walking, menus or reading), so real play is longer.

Targets (GAME_DESIGN §2.3, BUILD_BRIEF M15): deaths per zone rising gently; bosses 2–5 attempts; first clear ~3–4 h.
