# Owner checks — 25 things to try

Each line is one thing to do and what you should see. Tick them off as you play.

1. [ ] Open https://josephjackman.com/games/dungeonjoe/, click once for sound, and the title screen's endless stairs and music start.
2. [ ] Create a character of each of two different classes and see that their gear, stats and spells differ on the creation screen.
3. [ ] Play the Training Hall and do all nine stations (each sign turns gold when done); let the Instructor flatten you and see that you get back up instead of dying.
4. [ ] Stand inside a dummy's red-glowing stomp and dodge through it at the last moment: no damage.
5. [ ] Stand inside a blue (Piercing) telegraph and dodge: you still get hit; walk out of it instead and you don't.
6. [ ] Tap block just as an enemy's normal swing lands to parry, then hit it while it reels for a big riposte.
7. [ ] Leave the hall, rest at the first bonfire, and see your HP and Thermos refill; rest again and they do not (only dying refills it).
8. [ ] Die on purpose, then walk back to the blue Shardpile where you fell and take your Shards back.
9. [ ] Level up at a bonfire with your Shards and feel the difference in your main stat's damage.
10. [ ] Pick up a Rare or better item, compare it in the inventory (Tab), equip it and check the tooltip and your stats change.
11. [ ] Socket a gem at the bonfire's gem bench and see its effect in the item tooltip.
12. [ ] Open a chest that might be a mimic (hold F): sometimes it bites; attack the next suspicious chest first instead.
13. [ ] Use each of your weapon's three skills (Q, E, R) and watch the cooldown rings on the HUD.
14. [ ] As a Scholar or Cleric, swap spells in the bonfire's Spells tab and cast all three slots.
15. [ ] Beat The Caretaker: the name card, the phase change at half health (burning moss), the boss bar tick, the loot and the stairs down.
16. [ ] Go down to zone 2 and notice the new palette, enemies, hazards and music.
17. [ ] After two bosses, check that your Thermos holds one more charge.
18. [ ] Find Gary in a merchant room (zones 2–8), buy something for Stardust, then hit him three times and he leaves for good.
19. [ ] Press **Host a game** on the title screen, have a friend press **Join a game** and type your 6-letter code on another PC, and fight a room together.
20. [ ] In co-op, let your friend fall and revive their ghost by hitting it; check the revive ring fills.
21. [ ] In co-op, check that each of you sees only your own loot drops, and that your friend keeps them after leaving.
22. [ ] Use **Return to lobby** from the host's pause menu, let a third person join, and start again.
23. [ ] Rebind dodge in Settings → Keyboard (or Gamepad) and play a room with the new key; try the high-contrast telegraphs option.
24. [ ] Export a character from its card, import it into another slot, and continue playing it.
25. [ ] Reach The Abyss, beat The Landlord's three phases, watch the credits, and start New Game+ at the blue fire.
