# Balance report (M15)

> The generated numbers report (enemy and boss curves, time-to-kill per build, gems, weapons, spells, Shards) is
> docs/balance/report.md: `npm run balance:report` rewrites it in a few seconds, and every deploy run shows it on its
> summary page. This file is the curated write-up of the bot campaigns.

Produced by the headless harness in `tests/sim/`: `npm run balance` plays full campaigns (`tests/sim/balance.sim.ts`,
raw table in `docs/balance/campaigns.md`) and `npx vitest run tests/sim/bosses.sim.ts --config vitest.sim.config.ts`
runs the boss sweep (`docs/balance/bosses.md`). Both use the same
scripted bot (`tests/sim/bot.ts`) in four builds: greathammer (Brute), daggers (Duelist), caster (Scholar, spells
only) and cleric (Mace + Prayer).

## What the bot is, and what that means for these numbers

The bot reacts to telegraphs after 0.25 s, picks one of eight escape directions that clears every live telegraph
before it lands, runs to pockets, dodges Red hits and close melee swings with its i-frames, keeps a stamina reserve,
backs off when crowded, drinks below 40% HP, and levels up at every rest with all its Shards. It does **not** block,
parry, read attack patterns, kite bosses deliberately or learn between attempts. It is a weaker player than the
"competent player" the design targets, so:

- **Absolute boss numbers are not targets.** The bots rarely beat a boss in 12 attempts, even with 30 extra levels
  (sweep below). Extra levels barely change their results, which says their deaths come from mistakes, not stats.
- **Relative numbers are useful.** Every boss should need about the same margin over its expected level, and the
  attribution traces (which attack did the damage) show which attacks are unfair rather than hard.
- **Rooms are informative.** Rooms are mostly won; zone pacing and Shard income can be read from the campaigns.

## Findings

1. **Shard curve: on target.** Bots reach the zone-1 boss at level 10–12 against the expected 12 (GAME_DESIGN §2.3)
   after one clear. Later "level at boss" figures include the re-clears a stuck bot makes, so they run high.
2. **Pacing.** A first clear of a zone is 5–30 simulated minutes of pure fighting for these bots; with walking,
   looting, menus and reading, the 3–4 hour first clear (BUILD_BRIEF M15) is plausible for a competent player.
3. **Deaths rise with the zone**, steeply for the bots in zones 6–8: late enemies hit for a larger share of a
   character's HP (enemy damage grows ×3.45 by zone 8 while player HP grows ~×1.8 with the bot's even stat spread).
   A human who levels HP and wears late armor fares better; this is the main thing for the owner to feel out
   (docs/OWNER_CHECKS.md), and the place to tune if the late game feels unfair is the 0.35 damage coefficient.
4. **Two unfair attacks, fixed (D71):**
   - *Archmagister Tock, Arcane Volley*: at point blank all five bolts of a 48° fan landed — ×4 of a boss hit from one
     unblockable-in-practice "normal" attack. The fan is now 72° and he does not use it inside 120 px, so a close
     volley lands one or two bolts.
   - *The Landlord, Eviction Notices*: homing 80°/s at 250 px/s made the papers impossible to sidestep. Homing is now
     45°/s; the damage per paper is unchanged.
5. **Relative boss difficulty (sweep):** the Caretaker, Mother Spore, the Bloatking and Vexthorn sit together (bots
   leave them at 10–40%); Ignatius and Aurelia a little harder; Tock harder; The Landlord is the clear peak (bots
   rarely take him below 70%), which is right for the final boss, with the caveat in 3.
6. **Caster** builds depend on spell uptime; after the bot was taught to cast whenever a spell is ready its results
   match the melee builds in zones 1–5.

To reproduce with the brief's full sample (50 campaigns per bot), in PowerShell:

```powershell
$env:RUNS = "50"; npm run balance
```

## Campaigns (5 per bot)

Generated by `npm run balance` (tests/sim/balance.sim.ts) with 5 campaign(s) per bot. Each bot starts a new
character of its class and plays zones 1–8 in order: every combat, ambush and treasure room (a death sends it
back to the bonfire and it fights the room again, up to 4 times), rests at the anteroom, then fights the boss
until it falls (up to 12 attempts). It levels up at every rest with its Shards and wears better drops.
Bots react to telegraphs after 0.25 s, dodge Red hits, step out of Piercing areas and use pockets.
Times are simulated seconds of fighting (no walking, menus or reading), so real play is longer.

Targets (GAME_DESIGN §2.3, BUILD_BRIEF M15): deaths per zone rising gently; bosses 2–5 attempts; first clear ~3–4 h.
